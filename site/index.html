The sweet spot follows you.

How it works

A camera, an audio engine, and your own speakers

  1. The camera finds your head

    A camera near the screen or speakers measures where you sit, in millimetres from the listening position you set up once.

  2. The sound is tuned to that place

    The audio engine prepares what each speaker plays for where you are, so the stereo image and the balance stay right for you, not for an empty chair.

  3. You only notice the music

    Move, lean back or get up: what you hear stays natural, without anything drawing your attention to the system. When you leave, the room settles back to its sweet spot.

Setups

From a laptop on a desk to a hi-fi in the living room

Mac

Your Mac and its speakers

One app in the menu bar with the tracker and the engine inside. It balances what other apps play, such as Spotify or a game.

  • macOS 14 or later
  • Built-in or external camera
  • Signed and notarized
Raspberry Pi

A hi-fi or a home cinema

A Raspberry Pi with its camera module runs the tracker and the engine next to your amplifier. Stream to it with Spotify Connect.

  • Stereo up to 9 speakers, subwoofers and height speakers
  • Set up from any browser on your network
iPhone and iPad

Room setup in minutes

Point your iPhone at each speaker to place it in the room plan. Then recalibrate or pause from your sofa.

  • More precise with LiDAR (iPhone Pro, iPad Pro)
  • iOS 17 or later
Details

Made for real rooms

Several listeners

Follow just the person in the listening seat, or everyone in view, with the sound balanced for the group.

A camera that gets knocked

It recognises the room behind you and corrects itself when the camera is turned a little.

Your speaker layout

Presets from stereo to 7.2.4, or place each speaker yourself in the room plan.

Studio audio

Works with audio interfaces and Dante Virtual Soundcard.

Privacy

The camera image stays at home

The camera image is processed on the device the camera is connected to. Only your position goes to the audio engine. Nothing is sent to a server on the internet.

camera → image → tracker (image stops here)
tracker → x 12, y −3, z 40 cm → engine
engine → sound for your place → speakers
