Head-tracked sound

The sweet spot follows you.

Every recording is made for one ideal place to listen. TrueField brings that place to wherever you are, so you hear the full stereo stage, and even complete surround, from any seat.

Get early access Why it matters
Why

Hear the whole stage, not just the nearest speaker

Music is mixed for one place: right between the speakers. There it opens up into a stage in front of you, with the singer in the middle and every instrument in its own spot. TrueField makes that place follow you.

Stereo

The stage stays in front of you

Sit at the end of the sofa and the stage normally leans towards the nearest speaker, taking the singer with it. With TrueField the singer stays in the middle and the stage stays wide, whether you sit, lean back or change seats.

Advanced: surround

The whole space around you

Surround asks even more of where you sit. Five, seven or more speakers have to come together at one point to put voices on the screen and effects all around you. TrueField brings that point to you, so films, concerts and games surround you the way they were made, from any seat on the sofa.

Rather no centre speaker?

Many listeners leave it out and let the left and right speakers carry the voices between them. Normally that only works from the middle seat. With TrueField the voices stay fixed in the centre of the screen, wherever you sit.

How it works

A camera, an audio engine, and your own speakers

  1. The camera finds your head

    A camera near the screen or speakers measures where you sit, in millimetres from the listening position you set up once.

  2. The sound is tuned to that place

    The audio engine prepares what each speaker plays for where you are, so the stage and the balance stay right for you, not for an empty chair.

  3. You only notice the music

    Move, lean back or get up: what you hear stays natural, without anything drawing your attention to the system. When you leave, the room settles back to its sweet spot.

Setups

From a desk to the studio to the living room

Mac

Your Mac, at the desk or in the studio

A complete software solution: on its own for music, films and games, or inside your studio software while you mix in stereo or surround.

  • macOS 14 or later
  • Built-in or external camera
  • Signed and notarized
Hi-fi and home cinema

Part of your audio system

TrueField is made to live where your sound is made: on a computer next to your amplifier, or built into a streamer, a DSP, a preamplifier or active speakers.

  • Stereo, surround and height speakers, with subwoofers
  • Set up from any browser on your network
iPhone and iPad

Room setup in minutes

Point your iPhone at each speaker to place it in the room plan. Then recalibrate or pause from your sofa.

  • More precise with LiDAR (iPhone Pro, iPad Pro)
  • iOS 17 or later

Privacy

The camera image stays at home

The camera image is processed on the device the camera is connected to. Only your position goes to the audio engine. Nothing is sent to a server on the internet.

camera → image → tracker (image stops here)
tracker → x 12, y −3, z 40 cm → engine
engine → sound for your place → speakers
